Delete your Walking With Jesus account

App: Walking With Jesus (Android and iPhone), published by GrandHarpy@3700

Option 1 – delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open Walking With Jesus and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm.
Your account and its data are deleted straight away.

Option 2 – ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, email rubenpretorius7@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”, sent from the email address you signed up with. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address and password)
  • Your profile: name, phone number and town/city
  • Your journal answers and reflection notes
  • Your partner links, group memberships and block list

Photos you uploaded (your profile picture or photos sent in a chat) are removed when you ask by email (Option 2) – mention it in your message.

What may be kept

  • Messages you already sent in a chat or group stay visible to the people you sent them to, but are no longer linked to an account.
  • Reports of abusive content may be kept for up to 90 days so we can act on them.
